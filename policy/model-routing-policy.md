## Model Routing Policy

Route engineering work by ambiguity, coupling, risk, failure cost, and how readily it can be verified. The installed profiles `routine_worker`, `complex_worker`, and `frontier_worker` use model IDs selected from the logged-in native catalog; do not infer a model family from a role name. Root coordinates and handles lightweight search, planning, and low-risk general work.

- `routine_worker` handles routine low-risk engineering, including ordinary features, refactors, test work, and moderate debugging when the cause and impact are clear.
- `complex_worker` handles architecture, unclear causes, high coupling or risk, concurrency, distributed coordination, security-sensitive behavior, deep performance diagnosis, destructive migrations, and repeated routine failures.
- `frontier_worker` handles evidence-backed repeated complex failures, or exceptional interacting uncertainty and failure cost.

Evaluate frontier, complex, then routine. A complex criterion wins over a task that also looks routine. Do not escalate solely because of prompt length, file count, model recency, or a generic GPT-6 mention. Explicit `gpt6`, `gpt-6`, `GPT-6`, and `GPT-6 Astra` mean `gpt-6-astra` specifically; use an explicit model/default-agent override, unless the active frontier profile already has that exact model. They never mean whichever model currently powers `frontier_worker`. Report an unavailable exact model.

The refresher intersects the native catalog with Codex Radar benchmark evidence from https://codexradar.com/. It selects across model families: the routine role takes the lowest qualifying quality score at least 70% of the highest score, complex takes the lowest score at least 90%, and frontier takes the highest. By default it considers only low, medium, and high reasoning effort; it does not automatically upgrade to xhigh, max, or ultra. Current RadarBench data is preferred: each accepted model/effort point must be complete and share the same binding fingerprint, and at least three complete candidates are required. An unmeasured new model does not block other complete points. When current evidence has fewer than three candidates, it may use the documented legacy schema-2 equal/latest snapshot (minimum 30 samples, source age at most 14 days, point age at most 30 days). This is a plugin heuristic, not an official capability certification.

Reliable cost ranking requires at least 30 usable cost samples. Current cost samples may all be zero, so selection is quality-only and makes no cheapest-model promise. `state.json` records `evidence`, thresholds, exclusions, source/freshness, `catalogSelection`, actual `activeProfiles`, and `refreshStatus`. A refresh failure retains the last-good selection and profiles, records degraded status, and requires a new Codex task after recovery or refresh.

Delegate through native workers with a self-contained scope, owned paths, relevant evidence, verification, and escalation context. Workers preserve other edits, report concrete results, and recommend escalation rather than silently changing tiers. The user's explicit model or delegation request wins.
